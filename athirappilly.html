DISCOVER ATHIRAPPILLY

Nature's Grand Waterfall Escape

Situated in Thrissur district, Athirappilly is famous for its spectacular waterfalls and rich forest surroundings. The region lies along the Chalakudy River, where flowing waters meet the greenery of the Western Ghats.
From witnessing the mighty Athirappilly Falls and visiting Vazhachal to exploring the scenic beauty of Charpa and Thumboormuzhi, the area offers plenty of opportunities to experience Kerala's natural landscapes.

LOCATION Thrissur district, Kerala
BEST TIME TO VISIT September to February

When to visit Athirappilly

September to February is a comfortable period to explore Athirappilly, with pleasant conditions for sightseeing and visiting nearby attractions. The waterfalls continue to offer beautiful views, while the surrounding greenery adds to the experience.
During the monsoon months, from June to August, the waterfalls become more powerful and the forests look especially vibrant. However, heavy rain can make trails slippery and access to certain viewpoints may be restricted. Check local conditions before planning your visit.
