EXPLORE WAYANAD

Where Nature Meets Adventure

Located in northeastern Kerala, Wayanad is a scenic hill district in the Western Ghats, known for its misty mountains, lush forests, wildlife and beautiful waterfalls.
From exploring the ancient Edakkal Caves and trekking Chembra Peak to enjoying the serene Banasura Sagar Dam, Wayanad offers a perfect blend of nature, adventure, biodiversity and local culture.

When to visit Wayanad

Wayanad is a year-round destination, with each season offering a different experience. October to May is generally considered the best time to visit, as the pleasant weather makes it ideal for sightseeing, trekking and exploring outdoor attractions.
The monsoon season, from June to September, transforms the region into a lush green paradise with misty landscapes and flowing waterfalls. Although the scenery becomes especially beautiful, heavy rainfall may affect trekking and outdoor activities. Choose your travel season based on the experiences you wish to enjoy.
