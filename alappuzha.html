EXPLORE ALAPPUZHA

Where Waterways Tell Stories

Located along the Arabian Sea in Kerala, Alappuzha is a coastal destination celebrated for its intricate network of backwaters, canals and lagoons. Often called the Venice of the East, it offers a glimpse into the peaceful beauty of life along the water.
From cruising through palm-fringed waterways on traditional houseboats to exploring quiet villages, sandy beaches and local markets, Alappuzha brings together nature, culture and unforgettable experiences.

When to visit Alappuzha

October to March is generally the best time to explore Alappuzha, with comfortable weather for houseboat cruises, beach visits and sightseeing. The cooler months are especially enjoyable for discovering the backwaters and nearby villages.
The monsoon season, from June to September, brings refreshing rain and lush green surroundings. The waterways take on a beautiful atmosphere during this time, although heavy rainfall may affect boat trips and outdoor activities. Plan your visit according to the experiences you wish to enjoy.
