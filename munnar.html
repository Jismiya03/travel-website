EXPLORE MUNNAR

Where Nature Meets Serenity

Located in Kerala’s Idukki district, Munnar is a popular hill station in the Western Ghats, known for its lush tea plantations, misty mountains and pleasant climate.
From exploring tea gardens and Eravikulam National Park to enjoying the scenic views of Mattupetty Dam and Top Station, Munnar offers a refreshing escape into nature.

LOCATION Idukki district, Kerala
BEST TIME TO VISIT September to March

PLACES TO EXPLORE

Highlights of Munnar

Munnar offers a wonderful collection of natural attractions and scenic destinations. Explore beautiful tea estates, peaceful lakes, cascading waterfalls and mountain viewpoints that showcase the region's incredible beauty.

When to visit Munnar

September to March is generally a pleasant time to explore Munnar, with cool weather and beautiful mountain views. These months are ideal for sightseeing, visiting tea plantations and enjoying outdoor activities.
The monsoon season, from June to August, transforms the hills into a lush green paradise with misty landscapes and flowing waterfalls. However, heavy rainfall may affect travel plans and outdoor activities. Each season offers a different way to experience Munnar's natural beauty.
