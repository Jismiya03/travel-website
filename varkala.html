DISCOVER VARKALA

Coastal Charm with a View

Situated in Thiruvananthapuram district, Varkala is one of Kerala's most distinctive seaside destinations. Its striking laterite cliffs rise above the Arabian Sea, creating a setting unlike any other along the state's coastline.
Spend your days strolling along the cliff promenade, relaxing at Papanasam Beach, exploring nearby fishing villages or discovering the town's spiritual heritage. Varkala brings together beach life, local culture and a relaxed atmosphere.

LOCATION Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
BEST TIME TO VISIT October to March

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Choose the Perfect Time for Varkala

October to March is an ideal period to visit Varkala, when the weather is generally comfortable for beach outings, sightseeing and exploring the cliffside. The pleasant evenings are especially suited to enjoying the coastal views and sunset.
From June to September, the monsoon brings heavy showers and stronger waves along the coast. Although the surroundings become greener, rough sea conditions can limit swimming and other beach activities. Always check local safety advice before entering the water.
